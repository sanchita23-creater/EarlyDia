=====================================================================================
EARLYDIA: FEATURE SELECTION DOCUMENTATION
Non-Invasive Early Diabetes Risk Screening Through Accessible Insights
=====================================================================================

PROJECT PHILOSOPHY & NON-INVASIVE IDENTITY:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EarlyDia is engineered strictly as a preliminary non-invasive risk screening system.
It does NOT rely on laboratory blood-test values (no glucose, insulin, HbA1c, etc.).
Every selected feature is self-reportable, accessible without specialized clinical
equipment, and suitable for direct user collection via a future interactive frontend.

Total Features in UCI Dataset 891:  21
Features Selected for EarlyDia:     16
Features Excluded from EarlyDia:    5

=====================================================================================
SECTION 1: FEATURES USED BY EARLYDIA (16 FEATURES)
=====================================================================================

Feature #1: BMI
  - Category:            Anthropometric Indicator
  - Meaning:             Body Mass Index (weight in kg / height in meters squared)
  - Dataset Encoding:    Continuous / Integer: Range in dataset is 12 to 98 (mean ~28.38). Typical values: <18.5 Underweight, 18.5-24.9 Normal, 25-29.9 Overweight, 30+ Obese.
  - Why Selected:        Established primary risk factor for type 2 diabetes. Easily computed non-invasively without laboratory blood draws.
  - Frontend Collection: IMPORTANT FRONTEND INSTRUCTION FOR PHASE 2:
Users typically do not know their BMI directly. The frontend must collect 'Height' (cm or ft/in) and 'Weight' (kg or lbs) using user-friendly input fields and automatically calculate BMI using:
   BMI = weight_in_kg / ((height_in_cm / 100) ** 2)
The calculated BMI value is then passed to the model. Height and Weight themselves must NOT be added as model features because the CDC trained dataset uses BMI.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature #2: Age
  - Category:            Demographic Information
  - Meaning:             13-level discrete age category (_AGEG5YR BRFSS codebook)
  - Dataset Encoding:    Discrete Integer scale (1 to 13):
   1 = Age 18 - 24
   2 = Age 25 - 29
   3 = Age 30 - 34
   4 = Age 35 - 39
   5 = Age 40 - 44
   6 = Age 45 - 49
   7 = Age 50 - 54
   8 = Age 55 - 59
   9 = Age 60 - 64
  10 = Age 65 - 69
  11 = Age 70 - 74
  12 = Age 75 - 79
  13 = Age 80 or older
  - Why Selected:        Insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes risk increase significantly with age.
  - Frontend Collection: Dropdown with labeled age ranges (e.g. '18-24', '25-29', ..., '80+'), or a single age input box with a helper function mapping age to the 1-13 category.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature #3: Sex
  - Category:            Demographic Information
  - Meaning:             Biological sex of the participant
  - Dataset Encoding:    Binary: 0 = Female, 1 = Male
  - Why Selected:        Biological sex influences body fat distribution, hormone profiles, and metabolic patterns.
  - Frontend Collection: Radio buttons or toggle: 'Biological Sex' (Options: Female -> 0, Male -> 1).
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature #4: GenHlth
  - Category:            General Well-Being
  - Meaning:             Self-assessed overall health status (GENHLTH BRFSS scale)
  - Dataset Encoding:    Ordinal Integer scale (1 to 5):
   1 = Excellent
   2 = Very Good
   3 = Good
   4 = Fair
   5 = Poor
  - Why Selected:        Strongest single predictor of metabolic health in the CDC BRFSS dataset (correlation = 0.294).
  - Frontend Collection: Select box or button group: 'In general, how would you describe your health?' (Options: Excellent -> 1, Very Good -> 2, Good -> 3, Fair -> 4, Poor -> 5).
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature #5: MentHlth

  - Dataset Encoding:    Discrete Integer: 0 to 30 days (0 = 0 days, 30 = every day)
  - Why Selected:        Chronic psychological distress and depression correlate with metabolic dysregulation and poorer lifestyle adherence.
  - Frontend Collection: Slider or number input (0 - 30): 'During the past 30 days, about how many days was your mental health not good?'
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature #6: PhysHlth

  - Dataset Encoding:    Discrete Integer: 0 to 30 days (0 = 0 days, 30 = every day)

=====================================================================================
SECTION 2: FEATURES EXCLUDED FROM EARLYDIA (5 FEATURES)
=====================================================================================

Excluded #1: CholCheck
  - Meaning:            Had cholesterol check within the past 5 years (0 = No, 1 = Yes)
  - Reason for Exclusion: Measures healthcare utilization frequency rather than physiological or lifestyle diabetes risk. Additionally, 96.3% of respondents answered 'Yes', providing negligible discriminative signal.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Excluded #2: AnyHealthcare
  - Meaning:            Have any kind of healthcare coverage/insurance (0 = No, 1 = Yes)
  - Reason for Exclusion: Administrative and insurance access indicator rather than an individual physiological or lifestyle risk factor. Insurance status varies widely by nation and does not reflect metabolic health.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Excluded #3: NoDocbcCost
  - Meaning:            Could not see a doctor because of cost in past 12 months (0 = No, 1 = Yes)
  - Reason for Exclusion: Financial and healthcare system barrier metric rather than an individual health risk determinant.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Excluded #4: Education
  - Meaning:            Highest level of education completed (scale 1-6)
  - Reason for Exclusion: Socioeconomic demographic variable that serves as a distal proxy rather than a direct biological or lifestyle determinant. Excluded to prevent socioeconomic bias in clinical screening.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Excluded #5: Income
  - Meaning:            Annual household income bracket (scale 1-8 based on US dollar thresholds)
  - Reason for Exclusion: US-specific dollar income scale that induces user hesitation/privacy discomfort during screening, lacks direct metabolic relevance, and introduces geographical and economic bias.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 3: FRONTEND MAPPING & IMPLEMENTATION RULES FOR PHASE 2
=====================================================================================

A. BMI COLLECTION & CALCULATION RULE:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Users often do not know their BMI directly.
  - The Phase 2 frontend MUST collect 'Height' (cm or ft/in) and 'Weight' (kg or lbs).
  - The frontend MUST automatically compute BMI using:
        BMI = weight_in_kg / ((height_in_cm / 100) ** 2)
  - Height and Weight themselves MUST NOT be added as model features.
  - Only the calculated BMI value is passed to the prediction pipeline.

B. EXHAUSTIVE DATASET ENCODING REFERENCE TABLE:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Feature       | Type      | Model Encoding / Value Format
  --------------+-----------+-----------------------------------------------------------
  BMI           | Numeric   | Continuous/Integer (typical 12 - 98)
  Age           | Discrete  | 1=18-24, 2=25-29, 3=30-34, 4=35-39, 5=40-44, 6=45-49,
                |           | 7=50-54, 8=55-59, 9=60-64, 10=65-69, 11=70-74,
                |           | 12=75-79, 13=80 or older
  Sex           | Binary    | 0 = Female, 1 = Male

  MentHlth      | Discrete  | Integer 0 to 30 (days of poor mental health in past 30 days)
  PhysHlth      | Discrete  | Integer 0 to 30 (days of physical illness in past 30 days)
  HighBP        | Binary    | 0 = No, 1 = Yes (diagnosed high blood pressure)
  HighChol      | Binary    | 0 = No, 1 = Yes (diagnosed high cholesterol)
  Stroke        | Binary    | 0 = No, 1 = Yes (diagnosed stroke)

C. RISK SCORE & SCREENING TIERS FRAMING:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The predict_proba output represents an ML-generated risk score / screening indication.
  - It is NOT a medically confirmed probability of having diabetes.
  - Prototype Screening Thresholds (Heuristic Student Prototype, NOT Clinical Diagnostic):
      * Low Risk Indication:                 Risk Score < 30.0%
      * Moderate / Elevated Risk Indication: 30.0% <= Risk Score <= 60.0%
      * High Risk Indication:                Risk Score > 60.0%
  - Clear medical disclaimers must be displayed alongside any screening indication.
