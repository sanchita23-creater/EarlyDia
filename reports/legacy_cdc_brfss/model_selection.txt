EARLYDIA: FINAL MODEL SELECTION JUSTIFICATION
================================================================================

SELECTED MODEL FOR EARLYDIA: RANDOM FOREST

CLINICAL SCREENING RATIONALE & METRIC PRIORITIZATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Screening System Identity:
   EarlyDia is explicitly designed for preliminary risk screening rather than medical diagnosis.
   In risk screening, False Negatives (telling someone at high risk that they are low risk)
   are clinically far more hazardous than False Positives (recommending a lifestyle check or
   consultation to someone who turns out healthy).

2. Recall (Sensitivity) Prioritization:
   - Random Forest Recall:      0.7745 (77.45%)
   - Logistic Regression Recall: 0.7597 (75.97%)
   Random Forest successfully captures a higher proportion of positive risk cases,
   reducing missed early indicators.

3. Discrimination Power (ROC-AUC):
   - Random Forest ROC-AUC:      0.8220
   - Logistic Regression ROC-AUC: 0.8170
   Random Forest demonstrates superior overall discriminatory ability across all potential
   screening decision thresholds, capturing non-linear feature interactions between BMI,
   Age brackets, and general health indicators.

4. ML Risk Score & Prototype Screening Thresholds:
   The ensemble structure of Random Forest generates continuous score distributions via predict_proba().
   IMPORTANT MEDICAL & SCREENING FRAMING:
   - The model output represents an ML-generated risk score / screening indication,
     and is NOT a medically confirmed probability of having diabetes.
   - The initial 30% and 60% partition thresholds are prototype screening thresholds
     developed for educational exploration in Phase 1/Phase 2, NOT clinical diagnostic thresholds:
       * Low Risk Indication:                 Score < 30.0%
       * Moderate / Elevated Risk Indication: 30.0% <= Score <= 60.0%
       * High Risk Indication:                Score > 60.0%
