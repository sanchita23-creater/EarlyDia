6. DATASET LIMITATIONS & OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------
a. Self-Reported Survey Data: All attributes originate from the CDC BRFSS telephone survey.
   Self-reporting is subject to recall bias and social desirability bias.
b. Ordinal Discretization: Age (1-13 brackets), Education (1-6 scale), and Income (1-8 scale)
   are pre-bucketed rather than continuous natural units.
c. Duplicate Rows: 24,206 identical rows exist because survey participants with identical
   discrete demographic and lifestyle categories produce identical feature vectors.
   These are legitimate distinct individuals answering identical categorical questions.
d. Target Combination: Class 1 combines prediabetes and diagnosed diabetes into a single binary group.
   This makes the target suitable for general early risk screening.
e. Laboratory Test Absence: The dataset contains no direct clinical glucose, insulin, or HbA1c
   measurements, making it an ideal benchmark for non-invasive risk screening.
